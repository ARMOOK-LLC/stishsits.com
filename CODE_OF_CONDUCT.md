# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, especially in security and design review.
- Focus on what is best for the people who would use this.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Promoting tokens, prices, "returns" or trading in project spaces, or asking anyone for a wallet recovery phrase.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **info@stishsits.com**. Maintainers will review reports promptly and confidentially and may remove content or block participants who break this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
